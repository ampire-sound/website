amp.ire is a producer and developer working out of Germany. The music and the software come from the same place: a habit of taking things apart to hear what else they could be.

The plugins started as private tools — things that were missing from a session and got built rather than worked around. Alter Ego is the first one polished enough to release, and it is still shaped by whatever record is being made alongside it.

Everything here is made by one person. That means the answer to a support email comes from whoever wrote the code, and it also means the odd slow reply. Both seem like a fair trade.
